KSP CommNet Workbench

Signal strength, orbit tracking, occlusion analysis & relay planning for KSP

KSP CommNet Workbench is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later, with no warranty. You can get its complete source code, and are free to modify and share it, at the source repository. Inspired by Poodmund's CommNet Signal Strength Calculator & Antenna Selector spreadsheet. Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Squad, Private Division or Take-Two. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Questions, or want to talk privately? Contact the maintainer over SimpleX (no account needed). Security problems: see the security policy. Other bugs and ideas go in the repository's issues.

This is a free hobby project. If it saved you time you can support it on Ko-fi, or with crypto (addresses on the “How it works” tab and in the README). Never required.
