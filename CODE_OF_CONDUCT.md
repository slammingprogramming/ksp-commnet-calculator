# Code of conduct

This is a small hobby project and I want it to be a pleasant place to ask questions and share what you know about KSP.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to KSP, CommNet or programming.
* Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Disagree with ideas, not people.
* Assume good faith; ask for clarification before assuming a mistake was careless.
* Stay on topic in issues and pull requests, and do not post other people's personal information.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, spam and publishing anyone's private information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these
rules. To report a conduct problem, open an issue if it is not sensitive, or contact the maintainer privately over
SimpleX (the link is in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

This policy applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
